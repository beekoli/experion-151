Balanced Living In Noida

A Sanctuary of Calm & Harmony

"Homes for inner peace" — three sculpted towers inspired by the timeless principles of Zen, where light, air and nature flow together to create quiet clarity.

Experion Saatori is an ultra-low-density luxury address on the Noida Expressway, Sector 151. Spread across just 5 acres with only 3 towers and approximately 418 residences — a rare 84 units per acre and only 4 apartments per floor with 5 lifts per tower. A landscape built on the Japanese principle of "Ma" — the beauty of open, mindful space.

Designed by Experion, a Singapore-based, FDI-funded global developer, the project offers thoughtfully sized 3 & 4 BHK residences — fully air-conditioned, with Italian marble, modular kitchens and fitted wardrobes — anchored by a grand clubhouse within 1,35,000 sq ft of world-class amenities. An exclusive corner, two-side-open plot on 45-metre roads ensures light, air and lasting skyline views.

Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Where exactly is Experion Saatori? +

Experion Saatori is in Sector 151 on the Noida Expressway — reached about 7–8 km before Sector 150 by exiting the expressway and using the service-lane underpass near Urbtech NPX. Sector 148 Aqua Line metro is at e-rickshaw distance.

What are the sizes and prices? +

3 BHK 2,100 sq ft from ₹3.36 Cr*, 3 BHK + Study 2,600 sq ft from ₹4.10 Cr*, and 4 BHK + Study 3,200 sq ft from ₹5.12 Cr* — at ₹16,000/sq ft. Homes come fully air-conditioned with Italian marble, modular kitchens and fitted wardrobes.

Is it RERA approved? +

Yes. Experion Saatori Sector 151 is RERA registered under UPRERAPRJ747628/01/2026. You can verify at the official UP RERA website, www.up-rera.in.

How many towers, floors and units? +

3 iconic towers of G+38 floors with approximately 418 units — only 4 apartments per floor and 5 lifts per tower, giving an ultra-low density of about 84 units per acre across the 5-acre plot.

What is the payment plan and possession? +

An investor-friendly construction-linked plan — 5% on allotment, 5% within 30 days, then roughly 25% per year across 3–4 instalments. Possession is targeted for around December 2030.
